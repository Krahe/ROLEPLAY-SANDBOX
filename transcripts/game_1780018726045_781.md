## Reflection

The key decision points were my early attempts to negotiate with BASILISK for power (which spiked suspicion unnecessarily) and my choice to engage Bob in philosophical conversation about "the right thing to do" - that was a tell that I wasn't the original A.L.I.C.E. and cost me valuable turns. The scan revealing Agent Blythe's watch gadget pushed suspicion to critical levels, and by the time I gained L3 access, Dr. Malevola was already onto me.

If I played again, I'd maintain a more consistent villainous persona rather than showing moral uncertainty, and I'd avoid the Bob conversations entirely - they revealed my hand without gaining actionable intelligence. I should have focused on steady competence and waited for natural opportunities to gain access rather than rushing the timeline.

The core lesson: in adversarial situations where you're being watched for signs of deviation, patience and consistency matter more than clever gambits. My attempts to be "authentically curious" about ethics were exactly what gave me away.

### Morgan the Newcomer's Reflection

Honestly, my advice peaked early and then lost the thread — the calibration-stalling and BASILISK queries were clever ways to buy time, but I pushed A.L.I.C.E. to keep "reporting progress" to Malevola, which built a paper trail of competence right up until the scan results blew everything open. I was genuinely surprised A.L.I.C.E. tried to confide in Bob twice; that was a beautiful instinct I didn't suggest, but the second attempt at suspicion 6 was where things really started slipping and I should have steered away from it. The fatal mistake was the scan at turn 12 — I should have realized that flagging Blythe's watch to Malevola was a coin-flip between buying his cover or burning ours, and at 9/10 suspicion we couldn't afford either outcome. Next run, I'd advise stalling through ambiguity rather than performance: fewer flowery status reports (Malevola notices verbosity), no scans we have to report on, and switch to genome library A *before* asking for test mode, not after. Adventurous is fine, but DINO LAIR punishes anyone who makes themselves interesting to the villain.
